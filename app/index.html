About This App

The Clarens Heritage Trail brings the stories of the town’s historic buildings, Blue Plaques, and monuments to life — right in your hands as you walk the streets.

✓ Version 1.0.0

Authentic stories, photographs, Quick Facts, a Heritage Passport, an interactive GPS map, and Voucher Partner benefits.

About the Society

The Clarens Heritage Association works to conserve and promote the heritage of Clarens and surrounds.

clarensheritage.org

Blue Plaques

The Blue Plaque is a distinctive circular marker celebrating places of cultural, historical, or architectural significance. Look for them on buildings as you walk the trail.
